# P6 practice and timing contract

- `/plan` stores optional exact dates, unknown dates, or a year/month/dekad. Early means days 1–10, Mid 11–20, Late 21–last day including leap day. No midpoint is used. Cross-year periods retain their actual years; fallow is a distinct sequence choice.
- `lib/plan/refinement-adapter.ts` returns `exact-date-needed` for intervals at the exact-date engine boundary. No interval sensitivity simulation is implemented. The existing saved-field rotation editor remains an exact/typical-date tool; Plan interval drafts are not silently migrated to it.
- `CropTransitionRecord` in `lib/store/plan-draft.ts` records source/target crops, method, previous harvest and next sowing as independent exact dates/intervals/unknowns. Relay and intercrop retain reported overlap rather than moving dates. Post-harvest no-till and tilled are separate choices; unknown stays unknown. Interval or unconfirmed transition timing is `not-evaluated` by the adapter.
- Relay practice is locally reviewable information. There is no universal 7–10 day advice. The legacy calendar helper retains its thresholds for compatibility, explicitly labels them an unvalidated calendar screen, and does not validate biology or simultaneous hydrology.
- `lib/engine/compare.ts` gates all crop slots in a rotation containing actual overlaps or legacy relay establishment BEFORE water/ET, suitability, stage hazards and paddy handover run. Slot `evaluationStatus` is `not-evaluated`, affected values are null, and disease comparisons are omitted. Sequential mathematical models have not been validated for simultaneous crop extraction. The conservative gate also excludes otherwise nonoverlapping slots in that same cycle.
- Topsoil feel is a qualitative report: very wet/oozing, moist/moldable, dry/crumbly or unknown. Observation includes a field/local reference, date precision, depth or null, known texture or unknown, and now/at-planting/remembered context. Village references are not confirmed fields. It is stored in the existing local Plan draft key. UI icons are explanatory symbols; farmer-tested illustrations and agricultural translation review remain unavailable.
- Neither new feel records nor legacy wet/damp/dry answers initialize numeric theta or root storage. Numeric depletion entered directly remains supported; post-paddy model handover remains an explicitly assumed sequential model, never a guarantee of germination timing.
- Main-crop correction clears its residue answer and transition record. Residue fate maps only for the main crop: incorporated/mulched/removed/burned map to existing engine enums; merely kept remains unknown. No following-crop residue is inferred. Cover days continue to come from actual crop periods. Soil mechanism labels now honor explicit residue fate before legacy residue flags.
- Missing moisture, drainage, crop rules, interval support and overlap science remain evidence gaps. Field checks are required; no germination date or safe planting countdown is supplied.
