# National geographic coverage

`myanmar-boundary.json` is the unmodified simplified geoBoundaries gbOpen MMR ADM0 geometry, boundary ID MMR-ADM0-35516675, revision 9469f09; boundary year 2021. Source: OpenStreetMap via geoBoundaries. License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.0; https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright.

Metadata: https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/MMR/ADM0/

Download: https://media.githubusercontent.com/media/wmgeolab/geoBoundaries/9469f09/releaseData/gbOpen/MMR/ADM0/geoBoundaries-MMR-ADM0_simplified.geojson

The country boundary identifies locations elsewhere in Myanmar after the existing three regional polygons are checked. It is approximate, not a legal boundary. It supplies geography, not regional crop calendars, verified varieties or rotation templates. Elsewhere-in-Myanmar previews return templates-unavailable; current field assessment remains visible with unresolved ranking. No regional templates are copied to other climates.
